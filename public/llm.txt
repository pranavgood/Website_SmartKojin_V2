# SMART Kojin

> SMART Kojin manufactures automatic, on-site sanitary napkin incinerators for
> institutional buyers — schools, corporate offices, hospitals, malls, municipal
> corporations, and factories. SMART Kojin is a product of Smartenviro Systems,
> a Pune-based waste-management company with 10+ years and 900+ installations
> across India (ISO 9001:2015, ISO 45001, ISO 50001, CE certified).

## What SMART Kojin does

SMART Kojin is a fully automatic sanitary napkin disposal machine and
incinerator. Used napkins are loaded through a sealed bin; sensors detect the
load and trigger incineration at 800–900°C internally while the exterior stays
around 37°C. The cycle completes in about 3 minutes and reduces the napkin to
ash, with treated exhaust air and no visible smoke. Models range from 30 to
200 napkins/hour capacity, sized to facility footfall.

## Key facts

- Manufacturer: Smartenviro Systems (https://smartenvirosystems.com)
- Location: Pune, Maharashtra, India
- Product line: SK-30, SK-60, SK-200 (capacity in napkins/hour)
- Internal operating temperature: 800–900°C
- Exterior temperature during operation: ~37°C
- Cycle time: ~3 minutes per load
- Emissions: near-zero CO₂, ≤1 PPM CO/SO₂/NOx, dioxins not detected (SMART
  Kojin's own product testing, vs. traditional uncontrolled incineration)
- Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, ISO 45001, ISO 50001, CE
- Patent status: patent application filed for the smokeless combustion
  mechanism (not yet granted)
